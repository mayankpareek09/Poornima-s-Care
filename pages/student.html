👕 How Laundry Works
1Submit your request — Fill in your clothes list below and click Submit (Mon–Thu only, not on Fridays).
2Keep your bag ready — Pack all listed clothes in one bag with your QR code visible. Staff will scan it when they collect from your hostel room, so be available by 8:00 AM next morning.
3Washing, drying & ironing — Track each step live above. Takes 1–2 days after collection.
4Delivered — Once status shows ✅ Delivered, your clothes are back at your room.
