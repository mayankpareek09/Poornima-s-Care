👕 How Laundry Works
1Submit your request — Fill in your clothes list below and click Submit (Mon–Thu only, not on Fridays).
2Keep your bag ready — Pack all listed clothes in one bag. Staff will collect from your hostel room, so be available by 8:00 AM next morning.
3Washing & drying — Clothes are washed, dried, and neatly folded. Takes 1–2 days after collection.
4Collect your clothes — Once status shows ✅ Ready, collect your bag from the Laundry Room (Ground Floor, Hostel Block).
📌 Rules: 30 washes per year · One bag at a time · No submissions on Fridays · Minimum 3 items per wash
